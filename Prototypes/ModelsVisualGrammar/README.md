# FoldWise Models visual grammar prototype

> THROWAWAY PROTOTYPE — this is a visual decision aid for Wayfinder ticket
> “Prototype Models within the new visual grammar,” not production code.

Question: How should the approved comparison-ledger and inspector Models
workspace be restyled within Ember Edge without changing product behavior?

Run the isolated native SwiftUI gallery from the repository root:

```sh
./Prototypes/ModelsVisualGrammar/run.sh
```

The bottom review bar switches among three structurally different treatments:

- **A — Trace Ledger**: a balanced comparison table and inspector joined by a
  restrained orange inspection trace. Recommended: it keeps the scan path
  strongest while translating the approved visual direction most directly.
- **B — Family Bays**: Speech recognition and Polish become bounded work bays,
  with a compact management inspector.
- **C — Inspector Forward**: a condensed model index gives the inspector more
  room for recovery, progress, error, and destructive consequences.

Command–Left Arrow and Command–Right Arrow cycle variants. The review bar also
forces Light or Dark, Default or the exact 617 pt compact Models width, the
representative Baseline/Fallback/Progress/Repair/Error/Focus/Confirm states, and
Standard or Contrast+ borders.

Render the review matrix to `.context/models-visual-grammar-shots` with:

```sh
./Prototypes/ModelsVisualGrammar/run.sh --render
```

The fixtures are curated decision aids, not live lifecycle implementations.
They preserve the existing domain distinctions:

- inspecting never changes ASR model selection;
- ASR model selection, ASR model availability, and Effective ASR model remain
  visibly separate;
- optional downloads change availability only;
- Polish remains Mode inventory with no page-level selection;
- progress, cancellation, errors, recovery, and destructive consequences stay
  attached to the affected family or model.

## Verdict

Approved: **A — Trace Ledger**.

Trace Ledger won because it preserves the Models workspace's strongest existing
idea—the aligned comparison scan—while translating it directly into Ember Edge.
The inspected row's restrained orange ingress mark continues at the inspector
edge, making inspection legible without confusing it with the saved ASR model
selection. Fine borders, near-black or warm off-white opaque layers, compact
rows, SF Mono model data, and a quiet inspector keep the dense catalog readable.

The approved state grammar is:

- saved ASR model selection uses a checkmark, explicit Selected text, and the
  orange row trace;
- availability remains a separate State-column fact;
- a saved-but-unavailable selection and its Effective ASR model fallback remain
  simultaneously visible through a family notice, two-line row states, and
  complete inspector explanation;
- download and repair actions use orange, while success, warning, and error keep
  their dedicated semantic colors plus persistent icon and text cues;
- progress stays attached to its model, with cancellation shown only where the
  lifecycle permits it;
- destructive consequences use a focused confirmation naming removed storage,
  affected Modes, and raw-text fallback;
- keyboard focus uses the approved separated two-point ring, and Contrast+
  strengthens borders without changing hierarchy; and
- at the exact 617 pt Models width, the ledger and inspector stay side by side
  at their 340 pt and 276 pt resolved widths with compact labels that retain
  their complete meaning.

Family Bays and Inspector Forward remain in the gallery as throwaway review
context. They were not selected: Family Bays adds too much container hierarchy,
while Inspector Forward weakens the comparison ledger by giving detail more
space than the routine scan requires.
